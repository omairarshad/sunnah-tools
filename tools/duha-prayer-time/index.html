Duha Prayer Time Calculator

Estimate when Duha prayer can be offered based on calculated sunrise and Dhuhr or manually entered local prayer times.

How to find the Duha prayer window

Duha is a voluntary daytime prayer. Its time begins after the sunrise prohibition and continues until shortly before the sun reaches its highest point, when the Dhuhr window approaches.

Sunrise and zenith

Sunrise is not itself the beginning of Duha. Our suggested start adds 20 minutes, and the cautious finish subtracts 10 minutes from the calculated Dhuhr start. These are planning margins, not universally established religious thresholds.

Number of rak’ahs

Two voluntary rak’ahs are a common starting point. Scholarly views differ on the fuller range and preferred number. For high-latitude locations and local timetable methods, confirm the chosen mosque convention.

Worked example

If sunrise is 07:00 and Dhuhr begins at 12:30, the suggested planner interval is 07:20–12:20. This does not replace a local mosque or scholar’s guidance.

01What are Duha and Ishraq?+

Duha is a voluntary prayer after the sunrise prohibition has passed and before the sun reaches its zenith. Ishraq commonly refers to prayer near the beginning of that period, though terminology and practice vary.

The calculator begins with actual sunrise and Dhuhr start estimates, not Fajr or the moment the sun first appears above the horizon.

02Why are there 20-minute and 10-minute margins?+

The tool uses 20 minutes after sunrise and 10 minutes before Dhuhr as cautious default planning offsets. These are not universal legal cutoffs. The time for the sun to rise above the prohibited period depends on season and location; discuss locally observed practice with a knowledgeable scholar.

If your mosque publishes times, activate manual entry to use its sunrise and Dhuhr schedule. You can change the optional buffer settings, but a shorter buffer is not a ruling that the prayer becomes valid at that exact minute.

Worked example:Sunrise 07:00, Dhuhr 12:30, start buffer 20 minutes and end buffer 10 minutes produces a suggested 07:20–12:20 window.

03When is Duha especially recommended?+

Sahih Muslim 748a describes the prayer of the penitent at a later morning period when the sun has become warm. This indicates a merit associated with later Duha, rather than restricting the whole prayer to one fixed clock time.

For practical planning, choose a time comfortably between the early sunrise prohibition and the approach of Dhuhr.

Sahih Muslim 748a – Later-morning Duha↗

04How many rakahs?+

Two rakahs are well-attested. Reports and juristic guidance also discuss four, six and eight rakahs. If you pray additional units, do so in a way consistent with the guidance you follow.

No browser calculation can decide the acceptance of a prayer. The timing result is an aid to planning, not a religious certificate.

Sahih Muslim 720 – Encouragement to pray Duha↗

05Calculated city times versus mosque times+

The example-city selector uses coordinates, the Muslim World League calculation preset and the city time zone. Sunrise and Dhuhr do not depend on every setting that affects Fajr and Isha, but local timetables can still differ because of rounding, elevation, conventions and corrections.

Cities are examples, not proof of the user’s current location. Where high-latitude or unusual conditions make results unreliable, use authoritative local guidance.

06Common timing misunderstandings+

Duha is not Fajr, and it is not prayed the instant sunrise occurs. Dhuhr starts after the sun passes the meridian, so an estimated Dhuhr start is only a useful proxy for approaching the zenith.

A future-date estimate is not an automatically updated live mosque timetable. Check daylight-saving adjustments and the date you have selected.

Further questions

Can I pray right at sunrise?

Do not use the exact sunrise time as the beginning of Duha. Wait until the prohibited sunrise interval has passed.

Is the later part of the window mandatory?

No. There is a reported virtue in later-morning Duha, while the prayer has a wider allowable window.

Are the margins guaranteed worldwide?

No. They are adjustable planning choices, not universally agreed legal cutoffs.
