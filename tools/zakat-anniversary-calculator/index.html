Zakat anniversary calculator: hawl, lunar-year dates and complete guide

Short answer: A Zakat anniversary is a recurring Hijri review date after a relevant lunar year (hawl) has passed from the point recognised under your religious guidance. It is not 365 days after a salary payment, nor does a calendar alone decide that Zakat is due. Islamic Relief UK's scholar-reviewed hawl explanation describes the lunar-year basis.

What does hawl mean in Zakat?

Hawl is the passage of an Islamic lunar year relevant to many types of zakatable wealth. The Quran directs believers to pay Zakat, while classical hadith literature and scholarly jurisprudence explain the wealth thresholds and year condition; see Quran 9:60 and Sunan Abi Dawud 1573 for a report discussing a year and classical amounts (its transmission and application warrant qualified review).

A lunar year generally runs around 354 or 355 civil days. Because the Gregorian calendar is solar, a Hijri anniversary shifts earlier on the Gregorian calendar. Simply choosing the same date next year—for example, the same day in March—is not a reliable hawl calculation.

When does your Zakat year begin?

The starting point generally relates to when relevant owned wealth first met the applicable Nisab, but different schools consider changes in holdings or dips below the threshold differently. The Islamic Relief guide to when Zakat is due describes an anniversary-based review and practical recordkeeping.

For income, inheritance, a new gold holding or recently purchased investment, the fact that you bought or received that asset on a certain day does not automatically mean a brand-new, independent year begins for every item. Get guidance on hawl continuity and aggregation, particularly when your wealth fluctuated, Nisab was disputed, or you owe past payments.

Use this planner only after identifying a reference date under your chosen scholarly approach. It is designed for people who want a convenient recurring review reminder, rather than religious eligibility automation.

How the Hijri anniversary is calculated

  1. Enter a past Gregorian civil reference date supported by the Umm al-Qura calendar.
  2. The tool converts that date into the equivalent numbered Hijri month and day.
  3. It advances the Hijri year, finds each corresponding civil date and skips dates already in the past.
  4. If day 30 of a particular month does not exist in a future lunar year, the tool uses day 29 and clearly labels the substitution.
  5. Optionally view a ±1 civil-day illustration. This is not a prediction of a moon-sighting announcement or a claim of religious accuracy.
  6. Download all-day calendar markers for the next one, three or five reviews, then confirm your actual obligation independently.

The tool's time and date conversion is based on the platform's Intl Umm al-Qura calendar support; other calendar systems and community announcements may differ. Local Islamic days start at sunset, whereas the downloadable markers use a civil all-day date. A reminder has no automatic sunset alert.

Worked example: why a lunar-year anniversary moves earlier

Suppose a qualified assessment establishes that a person's initial Zakat review date corresponds to 10 Ramadan 1447 AH. Their next annual review target under the same calendar convention is 10 Ramadan 1448 AH, not automatically the same Gregorian date twelve solar months later.

The precise Gregorian day is calculated by the tool, and a local moon-sighting announcement could differ. This example does not establish that the person was over Nisab or that Zakat became due.

If a different lunar month has only 29 days in one year, a prior 30th-day reference does not map directly; the calculator labels its fallback instead of silently moving or displaying an impossible date. The planned date should still be reviewed with a qualified authority.

Calendar reminders, overdue years and data privacy

The downloaded .ics file contains annual all-day events marked as estimated Zakat reviews. Open it in a calendar application to organise a wealth assessment. It does not demand payment, establish arrears or contain balances, bank accounts or investment information. The separate text export provides a list of illustrative dates.

If you have not assessed wealth for several years, do not multiply today's total by the years in this tool. Your historical balances, each applicable year, asset treatments, the selected benchmark and any prior payments need to be considered. Frequently asked questions about Zakat anniversaries

What is a Zakat anniversary?

A planning date one Islamic lunar year after the initial qualifying Nisab assessment, subject to the applicable hawl and wealth rules.

Does hawl last 365 days?

No. An Islamic lunar year is usually 354 or 355 days, approximately 10–11 days shorter than a Gregorian year.

Do I always pay Zakat in Ramadan?

No. Your anniversary is linked to qualifying wealth, not automatically to Ramadan. Some people arrange earlier payments; check how these count.

Can I choose the date I received my latest salary?

Not automatically. The starting point is linked to wealth and Nisab under your school, not simply any income date.

What if my wealth dropped below Nisab?

The treatment of interrupted Nisab varies among schools. The planner cannot decide whether hawl has restarted.

What if I reached Nisab five years ago?

The planner projects the next dates matching that original Hijri month and day. It does not calculate overdue Zakat, debts or past eligibility.

Does a month with only 29 days cause problems?

If an original 30th Hijri day is absent in a projected year, the calendar planner uses the 29th and labels that adjustment.

Can moon sighting shift the date?

Yes. Umm al-Qura is a calculated calendar convention, not live local moon observation. The optional ±1-day control illustrates differences only.

Can I add the reminders to my calendar?

Yes. Download all-day .ics review markers. They do not assert a religious payment deadline and do not contain personal financial figures.

Does the app send notifications?

No. Calendar export only creates optional reminders within your own calendar application.

Is the reference date saved online?

No. No account is needed; the selected date remains in the browser for this session.

Does an anniversary alone make Zakat due?

No. Check Nisab, hawl, ownership, asset classifications and allowed liabilities. The date is only one input.

Common questions

Does a calendar date settle my hawl obligation?+

No. Nisab conditions and legal opinions must be checked before assuming Zakat is due.

Is a Zakat year twelve Gregorian months?+

A lunar year is shorter than a Gregorian year, so a Hijri anniversary generally falls earlier in the civil calendar.

Does this date tell me exactly when I must pay Zakat?+

No. Nisab, ownership, hawl and school-specific rules determine liability. Use the date as a reminder and seek qualified guidance.

Is a Zakat anniversary always 365 days later?+

No. Hijri years are around 354 or 355 days. Nisab and hawl conditions require separate religious assessment.
