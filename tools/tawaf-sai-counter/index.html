Tawaf & Sa’i Counter

Count seven circuits of Tawaf and seven lengths of Sa'i on separate counters while preparing for or undertaking Umrah or Hajj. Undo mistaken taps and keep progress on the current device.

Tawaf and Sa'i Counter: seven circuits, Safa to Marwah and complete guide

Short answer: Tawaf generally comprises seven complete circuits of the Kaaba, while Sa'i consists of seven one-way lengths between Safa and Marwah. The first Sa'i length is Safa to Marwah, the second returns to Safa, and the seventh ends at Marwah. Separate saved counters aid attention but cannot determine ritual validity.

Tawaf versus Sa'i: what exactly is being counted?

Tawaf is circumambulation of the Kaaba, with each complete circuit conventionally counted when returning to the Black Stone starting line. Sa'i is a distinct rite between the hillocks of Safa and Marwah. Quran 2:158 mentions Safa and Marwah, and Sahih Muslim 1218a gives a substantial description of the Prophet's pilgrimage rites.

How to count seven Tawaf circuits

Begin according to the appropriate rite and follow a qualified guide's instructions for intention, starting location and movements. Count one only on completing an entire circuit back to the starting marker. Continue to seven, using Undo if a tap was accidental. The app deliberately does not certify purity, physical direction, crowd restrictions or whether a circuit met the necessary requirements.

Sa'i route: Safa to Marwah for seven one-way lengths

Length one starts at Safa and ends at Marwah. Length two travels from Marwah to Safa, and so on; odd-numbered lengths end at Marwah. After seven one-way lengths you finish at Marwah. Seven round trips would be fourteen lengths and is not the conventional count for Sa'i. The counter displays the next direction based on saved progress.

How to use the separate Tawaf and Sa'i counters

Open the tool before starting the ritual. Increment Tawaf only after a completed circuit and Sa'i only after a completed one-way length. The counters stop at seven. Undo an accidental increment or confirm before a reset. Download a private snapshot if useful for continuity, while understanding that the snapshot does not prove the rites were performed.

Worked example: your third Sa'i length

Suppose the saved Sa'i counter reads two. The first length finished at Marwah and the second returned to Safa, so the next displayed direction is Safa to Marwah. After recording the third length, the direction switches to Marwah to Safa. Once the seventh is recorded, the tool says the route finishes at Marwah.

Important religious conditions the counter cannot check

The counter has no camera, location proof or ritual-verification mechanism. It cannot assess whether Tawaf was performed correctly, whether a break invalidated or changed a rite, what should be done after doubt, or whether special circumstances require a different treatment. Seek a qualified scholar or guide for such questions.

Crowd safety and accessible pilgrimage

Use the counter only when it does not distract from walking, crowd awareness or personal safety. Travellers with mobility needs should follow appropriate accessible routes and authorised staff guidance. A device can be put away; safeguarding physical health and safe movement is more important than preserving a digital tally.

Saved rounds and the progress snapshot

The existing browser storage key for Tawaf and Sa'i remains unchanged after the upgrade. Your two numeric counters are saved locally; a downloaded JSON snapshot contains only the count and version. Clearing browser storage or changing devices can remove progress. The snapshot is not a Hajj or Umrah completion certificate.

How to use Tawaf & Sa’i Counter

Track separate seven-round Tawaf and Sa'i counts; see the next Safa-Marwah direction, undo taps and save a private progress snapshot.

For Tawaf, increment the counter after finishing each complete circuit around the Kaaba, beginning and ending at the Black Stone. For Sa'i, count one length from Safa to Marwah as one, then the return from Marwah to Safa as two; seven lengths finish at Marwah. You can undo accidental taps or reset either counter. Both are stored locally in your browser.

Example: Count seven Tawaf circuits separately from the seven lengths of Sa'i.

How it works

Tawaf increments after each complete circuit starting and ending at the Black Stone. Sa'i increments after each one-way length; Safa to Marwah is one and the return is two. The seventh Sa'i length ends at Marwah. No location sensing is involved.

What to check

This counter is only a practical reminder. It does not authenticate the validity of Hajj or Umrah rites and cannot replace guidance for starting points, intentions or special circumstances. Progress may be lost if you clear data or switch devices.

A counter cannot check the proper conditions or sequence of rites.

Common questions

How should I count Sa'i between Safa and Marwah?+

Count each one-way walk as a separate length. Seven lengths start at Safa and end at Marwah.

How many circuits are there in Tawaf?+

Seven complete circuits around the Kaaba. Tap the counter once after completing each circuit.

Is Safa to Marwah and back one Sa'i round?+

No. Safa to Marwah is one length and Marwah back to Safa is two. The seventh length finishes at Marwah.

Will my Tawaf count stay if I close the tab?+

The counters are saved to browser storage on the current device, subject to your storage settings. You can also reset them manually.

Does seven Sa'i lengths mean seven round trips?+

No. Each trip from Safa to Marwah or back counts as one length. Starting at Safa, the seventh length finishes at Marwah.
