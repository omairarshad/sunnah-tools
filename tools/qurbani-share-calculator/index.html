How many Qurbani shares are in each animal?

The customary division is a single share for a sheep or goat, or a maximum of seven for a cow, buffalo or camel. This is discussed in Sahih Muslim 1318a, a hadith relating a seven-person sacrificial arrangement. Its direct setting was Hady associated with the Prophet's lifetime; established legal discussion extends share rules to the Eid Udhiyah sacrifice.

See the animal-by-animal summary from Islamic Relief UK. An animal's size and price do not override eligibility requirements. The application cannot verify its age, health or the slaughter process, and cannot turn a financially divided sheep into seven religious shares.

One-seventh shares versus funding the whole animal

These are different questions. Share reservation: if a provider quotes a whole cow at a particular price, each of its seven equal religious shares corresponds to approximately one-seventh of that price. Reserving three shares means your planned share cost covers three-sevenths, with four shares still to be arranged.

Whole-animal financing: if three friends agree to fund the entire cow, each friend may pay one-third of the price. But the payment division is not automatically the religious attribution of seven shares. Who intends which shares, whether some are additional voluntary sacrifices, and whether all participants meet the necessary conditions require separate confirmation with the provider and a qualified scholar.

The calculator uses minor-unit arithmetic (pence, cents or equivalent) to distribute rounding differences without losing or inventing money. This is a financial allocation, not an animal-price quotation or an actual booking.

Worked Qurbani example: three of seven shares in a cow

Assume a hypothetical total price of £700 for one cow. Dividing into seven sacrificial shares yields £100 per share. Three shares would be £300, while the remaining four shares represent £400. This is the result of the calculator's reserve shares mode.

In the fund whole animal mode, three people contributing equally to the entire £700 invoice would instead contribute around £233.33 each, with one person's contribution adjusted by a penny so that the sum is exactly £700. This does not by itself establish which religious share each person owns.

Different providers price individual shares differently or bundle administrative, distribution and slaughter fees. Enter the actual quoted total for the arrangement you are assessing, and check precisely what the provider means by a “Qurbani share.”

Animal age, welfare, intentions and eligibility

Age and fitness matter: common guidance lists roughly two years for cattle and five for camels, and at least one year for goats, with some differences or exceptions for sheep. Animals must be healthy and suitable, and humane slaughter must follow the accepted rules. Islamic Relief UK's Qurbani rules gives practical standards.

In the Hanafi school, Udhiyah has particular obligatory conditions for financially able residents; other schools commonly treat it as a highly recommended Sunnah. The exact position of family members, representatives, mixed intentions and shares donated on behalf of another person needs a knowledgeable adviser. A successful cost calculation proves none of these conditions.

When does Qurbani begin and end?

Udhiyah begins after the qualifying Eid al-Adha prayer on 10 Dhul Hijjah. There is a scholarly difference about whether the final permitted day is 12 or 13 Dhul Hijjah. Your provider should follow a recognised local view, verify the Eid announcement, and schedule slaughter accordingly.

Use the Dhul Hijjah Planner for calculated dates only. It cannot certify local moon-sighting decisions or supplier slaughter timing. Giving earlier to a charity can be an agency arrangement for a later valid sacrifice; confirm the organisation's process.

How Qurbani meat is shared and distributed

A commonly recommended division is to use one part personally, give one to relatives or friends, and donate one to those in need; charities may distribute all meat to beneficiaries. This practical pattern is distinct from the seven shares in a live animal. One-third meat distribution and one-seventh sacrificial participation are not interchangeable measures.

See the Islamic Relief UK meat-distribution discussion. The app does not calculate a guaranteed meat yield from a quoted price because animal condition, weight, processing and charitable distribution vary.

Frequently asked questions about Qurbani shares

How many people can share one cow for Qurbani?

Commonly up to seven share-holders. The underlying reported evidence in Sahih Muslim 1318a describes shared Hady sacrifice; its application to Udhiyah is established in common scholarly rulings.

Can seven people share a sheep?

No. A sheep or goat is one sacrificial animal/share under the commonly cited rules, not seven seventh-shares.

What does one seventh of a cow mean?

One of seven sacrificial allocations in the larger animal. If a provider quotes a whole cow, one equal share is normally one-seventh of its quoted price before any separate fees.

Can two people split the bill for a whole cow?

They can agree to finance costs together, but paying half the invoice does not itself determine valid religious share intentions. The calculator separates financing and sacrificial-share allocation.

Can a participant buy more than one share?

Extra shares can be arranged where the provider and religious guidance permit. The app lists the total selected share count; confirm intention and attribution.

When is Qurbani performed?

Typically from the Eid al-Adha prayer on 10 Dhul Hijjah, with differences regarding the end of the permitted period (12th or 13th). Confirm local date and practice.

Must an animal meet an age rule?

Yes. Common conditions distinguish sheep/goats, cattle and camels; consult your provider and scholar, including regional differences about eligible sheep.

How is Qurbani different from Hady at Hajj?

Hady is the sacrifice associated with pilgrimage circumstances; Udhiyah or Qurbani refers to Eid sacrifice more broadly. The shared-animal hadith is cited by jurists when discussing both.

Is Qurbani compulsory for every family member?

Hanafi and majority schools differ on its status and on who is responsible. Do not infer a personal obligation from a numerical share calculation.

Is giving a third of the meat obligatory?

A widely recommended pattern is personal use, gifts and charity, but detailed rules differ; charity providers may distribute the whole amount to people in need.

Why does the calculator show a one-penny difference?

It allocates indivisible minor currency units across selected shares so the split sums to the quoted total. It does not round every share upwards and overcharge.

Can I pay for a share through this website?

No. The calculator generates a cost breakdown only, with no animal booking, payment or verification of a charity or farm.

Common questions

Does an equal cost split ensure a valid Qurbani?+

No. It only divides money; participants, intentions and the animal must meet the religious conditions applicable to the sacrifice.

How many Qurbani shares can be in a cow?+

Under a commonly cited ruling, a cow may be shared by up to seven participants. Check eligibility and intentions with a qualified authority.

Can seven people split a sheep as seven Qurbani shares?+

The calculator uses the common distinction that a sheep or goat represents one sacrificial share. Sharing payment informally is not the same as establishing seven separate shares.

Does this calculator include animal prices?+

No. Enter the actual total quoted by your chosen provider, including any costs you want to divide.

Can every Qurbani animal be split into seven shares?+

No. The common seven-share arrangement applies to qualifying larger animals under relevant rulings, rather than every sheep or goat.
