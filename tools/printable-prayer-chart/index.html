Printable Salah chart: free 7, 14 and 30-day prayer checklist guide

Short answer: A Salah chart is a personal paper or PDF checklist with a row for each civil day and columns for the five daily obligatory prayers. Choose a 7-, 14- or 30-day period, print the blank table or export it as a CSV. A blank square means not recorded; it does not prove that worship was missed.

What is a Salah checklist and is tracking worship obligatory?

A checklist is a voluntary planning method. Islam prescribes Salah at appointed times, but no source makes using a printed tracking sheet an obligation. Quran 4:103 establishes the importance of prayer times; using a tickbox is a modern organisational preference rather than a prescribed ritual. The paper is not a measure of sincerity, acceptance or religious standing.

How to print a prayer checklist step by step

  1. Pick the first civil date for the chart.
  2. Select a 7-day, 14-day or 30-day schedule.
  3. Optionally write a heading such as 'My Salah chart'.
  4. Check the preview, which lists Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha for every date.
  5. Choose Print / Save PDF in your browser; scale or switch paper orientation if needed.
  6. Mark your own chart privately after prayer, or download an entirely blank CSV for editing.

The five Fard prayers and what each column represents

The five daily obligatory prayers are Fajr, Dhuhr, Asr, Maghrib and Isha. Sunrise is not an additional Fard prayer, and Witr/Sunnah prayers have distinct rulings. The checkboxes refer to your own acknowledgement of a prayer, not an automatic determination that it was prayed correctly or on time. Refer to the Rakats chart for Fard units.

What if I cross a month boundary or travel?

The generator advances Gregorian dates at UTC noon to avoid daylight-saving day-skips in the printed date list. It does not assert that a prayer's religious day begins or ends at civil midnight. When travelling or changing timezones, use a reliable prayer time calculation or a qualified local mosque timetable rather than inferring prayer windows from the paper date.

Worked example: a 14-day printable prayer chart

Choose 1 November 2026 and 14 days. The generator prints rows for 1–14 November and five blank prayer squares per row: 14 × 5 = 70 blank boxes. There is no evidence of 70 missed prayers; the boxes are blank until someone chooses to record them.

The same 14-day table can be printed as a one-page or multi-page PDF depending on browser scaling and paper size. The CSV provides the underlying date-and-prayer columns without personal marks.

Is the chart private and does the site save it?

The form generates a blank document in your current browser. It does not upload your prayer record or save names, marks or dates to an account. After printing or downloading, responsibility for protecting a marked paper or file rests with whoever stores it. An empty box is unknown, not a ruling that the prayer needs Qada.

Using a chart with children or family members

Parents can encourage learning through gentle, age-appropriate routines, but the responsibility and applicability of Salah differs by age and circumstance. Avoid turning tracking into public comparison or shame. Younger children may learn prayer names and sequence without implying that every unticked square means a religious failure.

Common mistakes with printable prayer charts

Do not confuse Fard, Sunnah and Witr into one obligatory category, mark an unrecorded date as 'missed', infer prayer correctness from a tick, or assume Gregorian date lines are a verified local mosque timetable. Use the digital tracker for voluntary private logging and the Qada planning tool only after checking which prayers are actually owed.

Frequently asked questions about printable salah chart

Can I print a weekly Salah chart?

Yes. Select 7 days and print or save to PDF.

Can I print a monthly chart?

Select 30 days. Calendar months have different lengths; a 30-day run is not every calendar month.

Is there an A4 prayer chart?

Use your browser's print settings to select A4 or another size.

Can I download a PDF?

Use Print and choose Save as PDF in your browser; this tool generates HTML for printing.

Does the blank chart show prayer times?

No. It is a checklist of prayer names, not a daily timetable.

Does it include Witr?

This version focuses on the five Fard prayers. Witr has separate rulings.

Is Sunrise a sixth prayer?

No. Sunrise is not a sixth obligatory prayer.

Does it track missed prayers?

No. Blank boxes mean only that no mark was made.

Are days adjusted for daylight saving?

Civil dates are generated using UTC noon and progress correctly across DST boundaries.

Can I export an Excel-compatible CSV?

Yes. Download a blank CSV and open it in a spreadsheet program.

Can families use it?

Yes. It is a voluntary private organisational aid, not a compulsory accountability score.

Does it save what I tick on paper?

No. Marks made on printed paper are never collected by the website.
