What is Nisab in Islam?

Nisab identifies a minimum amount of qualifying wealth. It is traditionally measured by gold and silver weights and is one condition used to decide whether regular Zakat is due. A person may own money, investment assets, business stock or precious metals: what matters to the assessment is the set of holdings and liabilities recognised under their applicable guidance, not only what they have in one bank account.

Sahih al-Bukhari 1405 contains the Prophetic report of no Zakat below five awaq of silver. Modern numerical gram conversions are derived from classical measures; some scholarly references use slightly different figures. We identify and consistently apply the 87.48g / 612.36g convention used by Islamic Relief UK.

Gold Nisab versus silver Nisab: why there are two values

Gold and silver have significantly different present-day market prices. The same person's qualifying wealth can exceed one threshold and remain below the other, which makes the scholarly reference choice material. Some authorities recommend the silver benchmark because it can increase the population who give Zakat; others rely on the gold benchmark in specified circumstances. The Islamic Relief UK discussion provides one contemporary interpretation, not a universal consensus.

The calculator displays both results at once. It does not choose whichever is easier to exceed, nor does it suggest that a user should pay twice. If the same entered net wealth passes one threshold but not the other, that is a comparison to discuss with your chosen religious authority.

What is hawl, and does reaching Nisab today make Zakat due?

Hawl is an Islamic lunar year relevant to Zakat on many forms of wealth; it generally lasts 354 or 355 days, rather than a Gregorian year. The determination of its starting point, treatment of wealth that fluctuates below a threshold, and income acquired during the year can differ by school and circumstances. Consequently, a successful threshold comparison in the browser does not settle a person's obligation.

Use the Zakat anniversary calculator for a planning date if needed, but treat it as a reminder rather than an official decision. Full Zakat calculations should include all applicable assets and only permissible deductions; see our Zakat Calculator.

How to calculate Nisab from market prices step by step

  1. Check a credible source for the current price per gram of 24k pure gold and silver on the intended assessment date.
  2. Enter both prices in the same currency. The selector only controls the label and clears prior figures when changed; no live exchange rates are applied.
  3. Multiply gold price by 87.48 and silver price by 612.36. The outputs are alternative threshold values.
  4. Optionally enter independently identified qualifying wealth and those liabilities permitted by your scholarly approach. This step is not necessary merely to see thresholds.
  5. Compare the net wealth to each result and review which benchmark is appropriate with qualified guidance.
  6. Check hawl and other rules before deciding whether any 2.5% Zakat estimate is payable.

Worked example: a household's qualifying wealth between the two thresholds

Hypothetical metal prices: 24k gold at £80 per gram gives 87.48 × £80 = £6,998.40. Silver at £1.20 per gram gives 612.36 × £1.20 = £734.83 when rounded to pennies.

Suppose qualifying assets are £2,000 and appropriate deductions are £200, leaving £1,800. This exceeds the illustrative silver threshold but not the gold threshold. If the silver reference is the proper one and the other requirements are met, 2.5% of £1,800 is £45. If gold is the applicable benchmark, the calculator shows a below-threshold result. This does not resolve which rule applies.

The market examples are deliberately invented, not 2026 quotations or an up-to-date UK Nisab figure. Market values change, so rerun the comparison with a rate verified for your assessment date.

What belongs in net qualifying wealth?

Cash, savings, eligible gold and silver, qualifying business goods, appropriate investments and money recoverably owed to you may need to be considered. Personal necessities and non-trading household goods can be different. The debt allowance also varies; do not indiscriminately subtract your entire future mortgage or long-term repayment schedule.

Frequently asked questions about Nisab

What does Nisab mean?

Nisab is a minimum qualifying wealth threshold relevant to Zakat liability. Reaching it arithmetically is not the same as confirming hawl or other conditions.

How many grams of gold are used for Nisab?

This site uses the widely cited 87.48g UK reference. Other recognised authorities may use slightly different equivalent weights; check your chosen method.

How many grams of silver are used for Nisab?

This site uses the widely cited 612.36g reference for silver. It is an illustrative standard from a contemporary scholarly source, not a claim that all conventions are identical.

Why does gold Nisab differ so much from silver Nisab?

The historical thresholds are defined by metal weights. Modern market values of gold and silver can diverge sharply.

Should I use the gold or silver Nisab?

Schools and institutions may use or recommend different references. The tool shows both rather than adjudicating which is binding in your case.

Do I pay Zakat on only the amount over Nisab?

In a conventional eligible-wealth calculation, the 2.5% is on qualifying net wealth when requirements are met, not merely the surplus above the threshold.

Does Nisab need to be held for a full year?

Hawl is typically a lunar-year requirement for eligible wealth, with school-dependent rules about beginning and end, fluctuations and new holdings.

Is a lunar year exactly 365 days?

No. Islamic lunar years commonly contain 354 or 355 days, subject to calendar method and moon observation.

Do I deduct a mortgage from my wealth?

Do not automatically deduct an entire long-term mortgage. Only enter liabilities your religious guidance permits, such as appropriate due amounts under that method.

Does reaching silver Nisab make Zakat immediately due?

No. You must also confirm hawl, relevant assets and debts, which benchmark applies and other conditions.

Is the current gold price automatically retrieved?

No. Enter a verified per-gram price for gold and silver in a single selected currency.

Why show two 2.5% outcomes?

They are alternative threshold comparisons on the same net wealth, never two separate Zakat payments to add together.

Common questions

Why are there two Nisab amounts?+

Classical gold and silver thresholds give different monetary values. Which benchmark applies can depend on scholarly guidance.

Why is silver Nisab often lower?+

The monetary price of 612.36 g of silver is often below that of 87.48 g of gold.

Does the threshold automatically mean I owe Zakat?+

No. Holding qualifying wealth, the passing of a lunar year, and other conditions still matter.

Why are gold and silver Nisab amounts so different?+

The benchmark weights and market prices differ, giving different monetary amounts. Your school or trusted adviser can help determine which threshold to use.
