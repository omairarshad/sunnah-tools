Friday Sunnah Preparation Checklist

Use a private Friday checklist for ghusl, clothing, khutbah, Quran and salawat, with direct Quran and hadith references.

Preparing for Jumu’ah

Friday is a weekly opportunity for worship, cleanliness, prayer and remembrance. This checklist combines well-known reports and links to their original sources.

Cleanliness and going to prayer

Friday ghusl is strongly emphasised in authentic reports, with discussion among jurists about its legal classification. Attend the khutbah attentively and avoid distracting others.

Surah Al-Kahf and salawat

Reading Surah Al-Kahf and increasing blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ are commonly encouraged; the reports about specific Friday virtues have different assessments. Study the evidence rather than treating every checklist item as obligatory.

How the tracker works

The checkboxes are stored locally for a single Friday and reset when you open the planner in another week. The checklist is for voluntary personal organisation, not a religious score.

01Jumuah, obligation and exemptions+

The Friday congregational prayer is an obligation for those to whom it applies, subject to recognised exemptions and individual circumstances. The checkbox list intentionally does not declare whether someone is required to attend.

Consult a qualified scholar about illness, travel, family circumstances or accessibility rather than using a generic web checklist to decide.

Quran 62:9 – Friday congregation↗

02Ghusl, cleanliness and fragrance+

Authentic reports strongly emphasise Friday washing, but schools differ in how they classify its legal status. Clean clothing and avoiding unpleasant odours show consideration for others at the congregation.

The checklist is a reminder to prepare; it does not decide whether someone may pray with an injury or needs an alternative purification ruling.

Sahih Muslim 846a – Friday ghusl↗

03Arriving and listening to the khutbah+

Arriving in good time helps avoid disruption. Attentively listening to the sermon is an important part of the congregation; avoid chatting or distracting others.

Follow the local mosque’s actual Jumuah start and khutbah times. Some mosques offer multiple sessions, especially in places with limited capacity.

Sahih al-Bukhari 881 – Early arrival↗

04Surah Al-Kahf and its reported Friday virtue+

Surah Al-Kahf is chapter 18 of the Quran. Reports about its particular virtue on Fridays have been graded differently; keep the distinction between reciting Quran generally and making an unqualified assertion about every report.

For reading convenience, the checklist links directly to Quran.com/18. The browser does not claim to verify that your recitation was complete.

Read Surah Al-Kahf↗

05Salawat and dua+

Sending blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ is encouraged in Islamic sources, including reports specifically discussing Friday. Many narrations also mention an opportunity for accepted supplication on Friday, with scholarly discussions about its timing.

A practical habit is to reserve an unhurried period for remembrance and personal dua without turning a proposed schedule into an obligation.

Sunan Abi Dawud 1047 – Friday salawat↗

06How to use this private weekly checklist+

The browser stores only a list of checked items for the selected Friday. No account, public profile or worship score is created. The entries stay on the device and can be reset or lost when browser storage is cleared.

The aim is practical planning: get ready, attend appropriately if obligated and able, and maintain worship with sincerity.

Worked example:On Thursday night, check when your mosque holds Jumuah. On Friday, set aside time for appropriate preparation, travel and worship without rushing.
