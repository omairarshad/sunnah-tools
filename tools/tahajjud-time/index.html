Tahajjud prayer: a practical guide

Tahajjud is a voluntary night prayer. The calculator above helps you find the final third of the night; this guide explains what that period means, how to pray, what you may ask Allah for, and where Witr fits.

How to pray Tahajjud, step by step

You do not need a special sequence of new prayer movements. Tahajjud follows the familiar form of salah. If you are learning to pray for the first time, start with the foundations of the obligatory prayers.

  1. Pray Isha first. Night prayer is offered after Isha and before the beginning of Fajr. Many people sleep and then wake to pray; the term Tahajjud is often used particularly for prayer after sleep, while qiyam al-layl is the wider term for night worship.
  2. Choose a manageable time. Use the calculator's last-third start if you hope to wake late in the night. If that is difficult, pray earlier in the night rather than abandoning voluntary worship altogether.
  3. Prepare for salah. Make wudu if needed, wear appropriate clothing, face the Qibla and make your intention in your heart. There is no required spoken intention formula for this guide.
  4. Begin the first rak'ah. Say the opening takbir, recite Surah al-Fatihah and then Quran you know. Complete ruku and sujud as in your usual prayer, then stand for the second rak'ah.
  5. Complete the second rak'ah. Recite al-Fatihah and Quran, complete the prayer's movements, sit for the final tashahhud and end with salam according to the prayer method you follow.
  6. Continue if you wish. You may pray another pair of rak'ahs. The Prophet ﷺ described voluntary night prayer in pairs. You do not have to complete a long session to begin. Sahih al-Bukhari 1137.
  7. Make dua and seek forgiveness. Speak sincerely to Allah about your needs, offer thanks, ask for guidance and forgiveness. You can use the Quranic supplications below. Outside the formal prayer you can make personal dua in your own words.
  8. Consider Witr before Fajr. If you have not yet prayed Witr, arrange it according to your school and circumstances. The number and method of Witr differ between schools; do not assume every Muslim prays Witr in the same way.

The two-by-two form of night prayer is described in Sahih al-Bukhari 1137; the Prophet’s practice of sleeping and then rising for prayer is reported in Sahih al-Bukhari 1146.

How many rak'ahs? And what about Witr?

Start with two rak'ahs if you are new to night prayer. You can add further pairs when able. Reports describe different details of the Prophet's night prayers, including an account of eleven rak'ahs inclusive of Witr; that report should not be treated as a universal mandatory count for everyone. Sahih al-Bukhari 1147.

If you expect to wake up

You may plan Witr for the end of your night worship, before Fajr, following your school of practice. Sahih Muslim 755b.

If you might not wake up

You may pray Witr before sleeping rather than risk missing it. If you later wake and offer more voluntary prayer, do not perform a second Witr that night. Sunan Abi Dawud 1439.

The legal classification, number of rak'ahs and performance of Witr differ among schools (including the Hanafi school). For a specific ruling, follow your trusted teacher or local scholarly guidance.

Duas you can make during the night

You are not required to recite a particular “Tahajjud-only dua”. A hadith highlights supplication and forgiveness in the last third of the night (Sahih al-Bukhari 1145). These Quranic phrases are meaningful examples to learn, reflect on and use in personal supplication.

The simple transliterations below are pronunciation aids, not replacements for learning accurate Arabic recitation. English explanations are original summaries of meaning; follow the Quran references for context. Rules for supplication within formal salah vary by school; personal supplication after finishing prayer is a straightforward starting point.

For goodness in this life and the next

رَبَّنَا آتِنَا فِي الدُّنْيَا حَسَنَةً وَفِي الْآخِرَةِ حَسَنَةً وَقِنَا عَذَابَ النَّارِ

Transliteration: Rabbana atina fid-dunya hasanatan wa fil-akhirati hasanatan wa qina 'adhaban-nar.

Meaning: Ask Allah for what is good in your worldly life and the Hereafter, and for protection from the Fire.

Read Quran 2:201 in context

For steadfastness and mercy

رَبَّنَا لَا تُزِغْ قُلُوبَنَا بَعْدَ إِذْ هَدَيْتَنَا وَهَبْ لَنَا مِنْ لَدُنْكَ رَحْمَةً إِنَّكَ أَنْتَ الْوَهَّابُ

Transliteration: Rabbana la tuzigh qulubana ba'da idh hadaytana wa hab lana min ladunka rahmah, innaka antal-Wahhab.

Meaning: Ask Allah to keep your heart upon guidance and to grant you mercy.

Read Quran 3:8 in context

For repentance and relief

لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنْتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ

Transliteration: La ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu minaz-zalimin.

Meaning: Declare Allah’s oneness and perfection while acknowledging your own shortcomings. This is the supplication of Prophet Yunus (peace be upon him).

Read Quran 21:87 in context

For beneficial knowledge

رَبِّ زِدْنِي عِلْمًا

Transliteration: Rabbi zidni 'ilma.

Meaning: Ask your Lord to increase you in knowledge.

Read Quran 20:114 in context

A short supplication for forgiveness

اللَّهُمَّ اغْفِرْ لِي

Allahumma-ghfir li — ask Allah to forgive you.

The words are found within a longer remembrance narrated in Sahih al-Bukhari 1154 about waking in the night. Read the full narration to learn its context; this short phrase alone is not presented as the complete narrated formula.

Questions and tips for beginners

Is Tahajjud obligatory?

No. It is voluntary worship. Keep the obligatory prayers as your priority. The excellence of voluntary night prayer is described in Sahih Muslim 1163a.

Do I have to sleep before Tahajjud?

Scholars often use Tahajjud especially for night prayer after sleeping; the wider category of night prayer, qiyam al-layl, is not restricted to people who wake after sleeping. You may still offer voluntary night prayer after Isha if you cannot wake later.

Is there a fixed Surah to read in each rak'ah?

No single Surah is required specifically for all Tahajjud prayers. Recite al-Fatihah and further Quran you know, taking the time you can manage. Do not assume you need to memorise particular lengthy Surahs to start.

What if the last third starts after midnight?

That is normal. The last third is calculated from Maghrib to the next Fajr, not from the civil midnight clock. For example, an 18:00 Maghrib and 05:00 Fajr gives a last-third start of about 01:20.

Can I pray after Witr?

Yes, further voluntary night prayer can be offered after Witr according to widely followed guidance. Do not repeat Witr in the same night. Details of timing and Witr practice differ between schools, so refer to your trusted local authority. Sunan Abi Dawud 1439.

What if I only have ten minutes?

Begin with two attentive rak'ahs, then make sincere dua and seek forgiveness. Regular, manageable worship is better than setting an unrealistic target and giving up. Leave enough time for Fajr.

Quran and hadith references

These primary references explain night prayer, its timing and the place of supplication. They are linked directly so you can read the full context.

  • Quran 17:79 — night worship.
  • Quran 51:17–18 — worship at night and asking forgiveness before dawn.
  • Sahih al-Bukhari 1137 — night prayer in pairs.
  • Sahih al-Bukhari 1145 — seeking, asking and forgiveness in the last third.
  • Sahih Muslim 755b — Witr early or late depending on whether one expects to wake.

Calculation method

Common questions

Is the last third of the night the same as midnight?+

No. Divide the interval from sunset to the following Fajr into three portions. Civil midnight need not fall at the halfway or two-thirds point.

What is the last third of the night?+

The final third of the period between sunset and the next Fajr, not simply the final hours before midnight.

Can I pray Tahajjud earlier?+

Night prayer is not limited to the last third. This calculator identifies a commonly preferred period, not a rule for every circumstance.

Can I enter Maghrib and Fajr directly from my mosque?+

Yes. Manual mode accepts tonight's Maghrib and the following morning's Fajr and divides that overnight interval into three equal portions.

How do you pray Tahajjud step by step?+

After Isha, prepare with wudu, face the Qibla and pray two voluntary rak'ahs in the familiar form of Salah, reciting al-Fatihah and Quran you know. Finish with salam; you may add more pairs and make personal dua afterward.

How many rak'ahs should I pray for Tahajjud?+

Two voluntary rak'ahs are an accessible starting point. Night prayer is described as being performed two at a time in Sahih al-Bukhari 1137; no single number should be presented as an obligation for everyone.

Which dua should I recite after Tahajjud?+

There is no single required Tahajjud-only dua. You may ask Allah sincerely for your needs. Examples of Quranic supplication include Quran 2:201 for goodness, 3:8 for steadfastness, 21:87 for repentance and 20:114 for knowledge.

Should I pray Witr before or after Tahajjud?+

If you expect to wake for night prayer, Witr is often planned toward its end; if you fear sleeping through, praying Witr earlier is supported by a narration in Sahih Muslim 755b. Witr details differ by school, and it should not be repeated twice in the same night.
