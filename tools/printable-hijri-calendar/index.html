What are the twelve Islamic months?

The Hijri months are Muharram, Safar, Rabi al-Awwal, Rabi al-Thani, Jumada al-Ula, Jumada al-Akhirah, Rajab, Sha'ban, Ramadan, Shawwal, Dhul Qa'dah and Dhul Hijjah. Each lunar month normally lasts 29 or 30 days, and the start of a local religious month can vary from a calculated civil date.

How the printable calendar calculates dates

The page converts a chosen Hijri month into weekday-aligned Gregorian civil labels using the Umm al-Qura convention. It does not assume every Islamic month has 30 days. The optional one-day shift only illustrates what an alternative community calendar might look like and is not a moon-sighting prediction.

How to print an A4 Islamic calendar

Choose the Hijri year and month, use Previous or Next to navigate, check the Monday-first weekday grid and displayed Gregorian labels, then use your browser's print controls to select A4 or another paper size. Save as PDF through the browser print dialog. A downloadable CSV also contains all 29 or 30 days with their two date labels.

What are the White Days (Ayyam al-Bid)?

The 13th, 14th and 15th of many Hijri months are known as the White Days and are often associated with voluntary fasting, as described in reported Sunnah. The calendar highlights these dates for reference. Ramadan is obligatory fasting rather than ordinary voluntary White Days, and 13 Dhul Hijjah falls in the Tashreeq days with particular fasting restrictions.

Worked example: a 29-day Hijri month

Suppose the selected calculated month contains 29 days. The grid prints exactly those days, with weekday placeholders before the first and no fabricated 30th. A CSV contains a heading row plus 29 dated rows. If a different calendar begins one day later, an illustrative shift changes displayed civil labels without pretending the canonical Umm al-Qura dates changed.

Do Hijri dates begin at sunset?

Yes, in religious observance the Hijri date traditionally starts with local sunset, although a computer civil-date grid shows the daytime date. A Gregorian Saturday box can therefore cover a religious date that began Friday evening. The calculator does not know a user's birth time, sunset or official local moon sighting.

Printable Hijri calendar versus a Gregorian calendar

A Gregorian month follows fixed solar calendar rules, while a Hijri month follows the lunar calendar and shifts through the Gregorian seasons. Both are useful for planning; the chosen system should always be named when giving a significant date. The downloadable date list is a calculated reference, not a national holiday schedule.

Avoid treating printed estimated dates as declarations

Do not rely solely on a printed calculation for the first Ramadan fast, the Eid prayer or specific Hajj rites. Consult your local community and relevant official authority near the occasion. Keep the printout labelled as calculated Umm al-Qura rather than removing the caution during sharing.
