What is Zakat al-Fitr and why is it given?

Zakat al-Fitr, also called Fitrana or Sadaqat al-Fitr, is charity associated with the close of Ramadan and the arrival of Eid al-Fitr. It is distinct from the yearly Zakat al-Mal on wealth and from compensations connected to missed fasts. Its purpose includes providing for people in need at Eid. Ibn Abbas's narration describes purification and feeding the poor in Sunan Abi Dawud 1609.

Sahih al-Bukhari 1503 reports Ibn Umar describing the prescribed food amount for Muslims, young and old, male and female, and the timing before Eid prayer. The wording concerns food, not a timeless price in pounds. Food prices, currencies and delivery choices change.

Who should be included in a household Fitrana calculation?

First establish the financial conditions and on whose behalf you are responsible under the school you follow. The hadith includes children; a parent or guardian may make arrangements on their behalf. Other dependency and family circumstances require clarification. Some scholars require a surplus of food over the Eid day and night, while Hanafi discussions use a Nisab-related test. The application deliberately does not turn household size into a religious verdict.

The calculator splits the count into adults and children so you can review the total without collecting names, ages or family profiles. The rate applies to the combined count you enter. A parent with two children can check three people without pretending the calculator knows whether they are all legally included.

One sa' of food, wheat and dates: why rates can differ

The traditional measure is a sa', a measure of volume associated with staple food. A kilogram equivalent differs by the food's density, so the source narration should not be converted into a universal precise weight for every food. Charities may publish monetary equivalents for local food packages, wheat, dates, rice or particular distribution arrangements.

Some scholars allow giving a monetary equivalent, while others emphasise delivering food. A charity receiving money as an agent to purchase and distribute food is also a question of permissible arrangements and timing. For a practical UK example of how one organisation calculates a current figure, consult Islamic Relief UK's Zakat al-Fitr explanation. We do not make that charity's current rate the automatic value for every reader.

How to use the Fitrana calculator step by step

  1. Confirm who is included and whether the obligation applies to your household under relevant guidance.
  2. Check the food or cash method your mosque or scholar accepts, and obtain an up-to-date rate from the recipient charity or provider.
  3. Choose a currency and enter the adults, children and verified per-person rate. Changing currency clears monetary amounts rather than silently converting them.
  4. If useful, enter a different charity's documented rate purely for comparison; do not combine the two into one price.
  5. Keep optional voluntary Sadaqah separate from the calculated Fitrana subtotal.
  6. Download or print a conditional estimate and arrange payment before the appropriate local delivery deadline.

Worked example: Fitrana for a family of four

Hypothetical rates only: Two adults and two children give 4 people. At an invented £5 per person, the Fitrana subtotal is 4 × £5 = £20. Adding an optional £2 voluntary donation makes £22 to arrange, but the Fitrana component remains £20.

If an alternative valid local provider quoted an illustrative £7 per person, its 4 × £7 = £28 subtotal would be a different estimate. This comparison is neither a current market quote nor a religious recommendation of one method over another.

For a different household or rate, use the calculator above. If your local authority treats a specific dependent differently, adjust the count only after obtaining guidance.

When should Fitrana be paid and distributed?

The hadith's time instruction is before people go out to the Eid prayer. A transfer just before the prayer may not reach the poor on time if an intermediary needs processing time. Ask when your receiving organisation stops accepting payments for that Ramadan's distribution and how it meets the religious timing requirements.

The Eid date is subject to local moon-sighting decisions. Our Ramadan Timetable helps plan illustrative dates, but it is not a substitute for an announced Eid or your local prayer time. If you have already missed a deadline, consult qualified guidance rather than assuming the calculator settles the status of a late payment.

Fitrana versus Fidya, Kaffarah and annual Zakat

Fitrana

End-of-Ramadan giving linked to people and the Eid deadline, with a prescribed food basis.

Fidya

Specific compensatory feeding in eligible fasting circumstances. See the Fidya guide.

Kaffarah

Expiation rules for particular cases such as an oath or specific Ramadan violations. See Kaffarah.

Zakat al-Mal

Annual qualifying-wealth assessment involving Nisab and hawl. See the Zakat Calculator.

Frequently asked questions about Fitrana

Is Zakat al-Fitr the same as regular Zakat?

No. Zakat al-Fitr is the distinct end-of-Ramadan duty linked to Eid, whereas annual Zakat al-Mal relates to qualifying wealth, Nisab and hawl.

Do I include my children and babies?

Sahih al-Bukhari 1503 names young and old Muslims. How responsibility is assigned to adults and guardians depends on the applicable school and household situation; consult your recognised authority.

What amount is Fitrana in 2026?

There is no permanent global currency rate. A charity may publish a particular equivalent for that year and locality. Enter its verified amount per person rather than treating the worked example as a quote.

Is the amount one sa or a fixed number of kilograms?

The hadith mentions one sa of food, a historical volume measure. Converting to an exact modern weight depends on the food and method; do not assume all foods have the same weight.

Can I pay money rather than food?

Schools differ on cash substitution and agent arrangements. Use a qualified local authority and a trusted provider that explains how it delivers the obligation.

When is the deadline?

The Prophetic report says before people leave for Eid prayer. Collection cutoffs from charities may occur earlier; confirm delivery timing and your local Eid announcement.

Can I pay early in Ramadan?

Practices and permitted time windows vary. Some charities collect during Ramadan to distribute in time; verify the method and timing your authority follows.

Do I need a certain Nisab to owe Fitrana?

The Hanafi approach and the majority approach differ on financial eligibility. This calculator does not determine liability from a household count.

Does the extra donation count as Fitrana?

The optional extra is displayed separately as voluntary Sadaqah, not an increase in the prescribed per-person Fitrana amount.

Does choosing USD or GBP exchange the rate?

No. Currency selection only changes the unit label and clears the old financial fields. Supply a verified amount in the newly selected currency.

Can I use an older charity rate?

Recheck the publication date and your locality. Old rates and unsupported cost conversions may be inaccurate for the current Ramadan.

Are my financial numbers stored?

No. The calculator processes the entries locally, and any summary is generated on your device. No transfer or payment takes place.
