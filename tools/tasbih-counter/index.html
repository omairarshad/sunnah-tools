What is Tasbih and why use a counter?

Tasbih commonly describes glorifying Allah, especially through phrases such as SubhanAllah. A digital counter is only an optional memory aid, like fingers or beads; it is not a ritual requirement or a measure of how much worship is accepted. The Quran encourages plentiful remembrance (Quran 33:41). Use a trusted narrated practice when attaching a prescribed count or virtue to a phrase.

33 SubhanAllah, 33 Alhamdulillah and 34 Allahu Akbar

One reported form of remembrance after obligatory Salah uses 33 repetitions of SubhanAllah, 33 of Alhamdulillah and 34 of Allahu Akbar, for 100 in total. See Sahih Muslim 596a and related narrations. The app gives this its own selectable pattern and moves between each phrase only after the user completes a stage.

Alternative 33/33/33 plus the Tawhid declaration

Sahih Muslim 597a reports 33 repetitions of each of the three phrases, followed by a final statement of Allah's oneness to complete one hundred. The app shows the last declaration as its own step, with a link to the full exact wording, rather than treating the short phrase 'La ilaha illallah' as a complete quotation of the narration.

How to use the online counter step by step

  1. Choose free counting or one of the narrated after-Salah presets.
  2. Tap Count or press Space when focus is outside a form field or button.
  3. Use Undo for a mistaken tap.
  4. For a structured preset, finish the displayed target before choosing Next phrase.
  5. Complete the final stage to record one chosen cycle; do not treat that number as religious merit.
  6. Use Reset if you want to clear the local count and completed-cycle record.

Worked example: completing the 33/33/34 pattern

After selecting 33/33/34, count SubhanAllah 33 times, then Alhamdulillah 33 times, then Allahu Akbar 34 times. The arithmetic is 33 + 33 + 34 = 100. The tool asks you to advance between phrases, so reaching 33 once does not automatically claim that all three sections were completed.

Choosing free counting and a personal goal such as 99 is a different activity: it does not turn that number into an independently authenticated prescription.

Keyboard access, undo, cycle totals and privacy

Spacebar counting excludes inputs, buttons, textareas, editable text and repeated keypresses to avoid accidental tallies. Count, stage and chosen cycles stay in the current browser. An older free-counter value is migrated when there is no current record. Switching between patterns clears the current phrase's count while preserving the recorded number of completed personal cycles.

Are Tasbih beads or digital counters required?

No particular digital counter is required in order to remember Allah. Scholars discuss preferred counting methods and the use of fingers or beads. A counter may help some people concentrate, but sincerity, correct words and appropriate practice are distinct from the tally. A free target of 100 is not proof of a specified Sunnah for every phrase.

Avoid mixing source-based dhikr counts

Do not add the 33/33/34 and 33/33/33-plus-declaration patterns together as though one newly invented total were prescribed. Do not assume an unverified internet quote promises a reward for every tap. Keep source and setting attached to the chosen dhikr, and consult a qualified teacher about the reported forms.

Frequently asked questions about tasbih counter

Can I use the Tasbih counter on mobile?

Yes. Tap the main counting control on a smartphone or tablet.

Can I count with the Space key?

Yes, outside inputs and interactive controls.

What is 33/33/34?

A reported after-Salah remembrance pattern with 100 repetitions across three phrases.

What is the 33/33/33 variant?

Another report adds a final Tawhid declaration after ninety-nine repetitions.

Does the app recite dhikr for me?

No. It counts your own taps and does not play or replace recitation.

Can I undo a count?

Yes, one step at a time within the current phrase.

Does it automatically change phrases?

No. You explicitly choose to advance after the current goal.

Can I use any personal target?

Free mode offers optional 33, 99, 100 or 1000 personal counting goals, not prescribed counts for every dhikr.

Will my count persist?

It uses local browser storage and can disappear if that storage is cleared.

The older free-counter value can be used when no newer session exists.

Do completed cycles prove accepted worship?

No. They are personal numbers, not a theological judgment.

Can I reset everything?

Yes, with confirmation before clearing the stored tally.

How to use Digital Tasbih

Tap or use your keyboard to track a dhikr tally at your own pace.

Your count is saved locally in the browser, with optional target reminders. You do not need to register or upload prayer records.

Example: Set a count target, tap to record dhikr, then undo any accidental extra tap.

How it works

Your count is stored only in your browser’s local storage so it can be restored when you return using the same device and browser. It is not synced to an account.

What to check

Your count may disappear if you erase browser storage or switch devices; it is not automatically backed up.

A counter assists memory; it does not establish a prescribed repetition number.

Common questions

Will my tally remain after closing the page?+

Usually yes, on the same device unless you clear browser site data or use private browsing.

Can I use the tasbih counter on mobile?+

Yes. The interactive counter works on mobile screens as well as desktop browsers.

Does the counter store my dhikr on a server?+

No. The tally is stored locally in your browser, not in a user account.

What happens to my Tasbih count if I clear browser data?+

Counts saved locally can be lost when site storage is deleted or a private session ends. A different device does not automatically share that progress.
