What is Kaffarah, and why does the kind of case matter?

Kaffarah refers to expiation in specified circumstances. A deliberate binding oath, an ordinary promise, a fast missed through an exemption, accidental breaking of a fast and the act described in a particular Ramadan hadith may have different legal outcomes. You cannot identify the right path merely from the number of days or a cash amount.

The two main sources used by this page concern separate events. The Quran directly addresses deliberate oaths in 5:89. The hadith in Bukhari 1936 describes one man's intercourse while fasting in Ramadan and his conversation with the Prophet ﷺ. They are not two price lists for the same obligation. Every selection in the calculator is an illustration conditional on outside guidance, never a finding of guilt, a binding expiation or a recommendation to pay a particular charity.

Kaffarah for breaking an oath: feeding, clothing and fasting

Quran 5:89 distinguishes unintentional or meaningless expressions from deliberate oaths. It speaks of feeding ten needy people on the basis of ordinary family food, or clothing them, or freeing a slave. For those who do not have the means for the applicable listed alternatives, the verse mentions fasting for three days.

A calculator cannot tell whether a particular statement was a binding oath, whether an oath was deliberately broken, if several statements create one or multiple liabilities, or whether a monetary substitute is accepted. Obtain a qualified determination on those issues first.

Kaffarah for a specific Ramadan fast violation: what does Bukhari 1936 report?

In Sahih al-Bukhari 1936, a man reported having sexual intercourse while fasting in Ramadan. The narrated exchange asks whether he can free a slave, then whether he can fast two successive months, then whether he can feed sixty needy people. The same narration goes on to discuss the man's poverty and a basket of dates.

That full context matters. The narration is not evidence that every missed fast automatically incurs feeding sixty people, that anyone may simply choose the cheapest option or that two successive lunar months always equal exactly sixty Gregorian days. The act, intention, exemption, ability, remedy order and relevant school of law all matter. Some schools differ on which Ramadan fast-breaking acts incur this type of expiation.

For ordinary days to be made up, use the Qada Tracker. For feeding compensation in particular inability-to-fast circumstances, read the Fidya guide. Do not import a number from either as a Kaffarah obligation without qualified advice.

Kaffarah for oaths and Ramadan FAQs

What is Kaffarah in Islam?

Kaffarah is an expiation relevant to specified acts under relevant Quranic and prophetic sources. A broken deliberate oath and a Ramadan fasting violation are distinct legal questions, not interchangeable calculator modes.

What does Quran 5:89 say about a broken oath?

The verse discusses feeding ten needy people from normal family food, clothing ten needy people or freeing a slave; if unable to use the applicable means, it mentions fasting three days. Qualified guidance is needed on details and precedence.

Do all broken promises require Kaffarah?

No automatic conclusion follows from everyday use of the word promise. Whether a binding deliberate oath existed, what was said and how it was broken require case-by-case religious assessment.

Is oath Kaffarah always fasting three days?

No. Quran 5:89 places fasting after the listed alternatives when a person lacks the means. A calculator cannot decide the ability threshold or the details of the applicable school.

Is there a fixed cash amount for Kaffarah?

No universal number can be safely inferred. The feeding or clothing method, local costs and whether money may substitute for the prescribed form vary. The calculator requires a user-supplied verified unit cost.

Does every broken Ramadan fast require feeding 60 people?

No. Sahih al-Bukhari 1936 reports a specific intercourse-while-fasting Ramadan case. Do not apply its 60-person feeding stage to all missed, deliberately broken or accidentally interrupted fasts.

What are the steps in the Ramadan report?

The hadith reports questions about freeing a slave, then fasting two successive months and then feeding sixty needy people in the man’s circumstances, with further context about poverty. It is not a one-click universal decision tree.

Is the Ramadan fasting alternative exactly 60 days?

The narration says two successive months, not an invariant 60 Gregorian days. Lunar months may have 29 or 30 days; the actual legal counting requires qualified guidance.

Are multiple broken oaths automatically separate obligations?

No. Repetitions, intentions and the form of each oath can affect the ruling. The calculator’s distinct-case count is an illustrative input, not a verdict.

Can a cash payment always replace feeding or clothing?

No. The validity of a monetary substitute depends on the situation and religious authority or school followed. A cost estimate does not authorise such a substitution.

Can I compare oath feeding and clothing?

Yes. The tool shows ten recipients per hypothetical distinct oath for either illustration, using a locally verified cost per recipient. It does not decide which remedy is available to you.

Does the Kaffarah calculator save my financial details?

No. Rates and scenarios are held only in the current page state. An optional text summary is generated on your device; no charity donation or payment occurs.

No. The event, relevant legal conditions, ability to use earlier remedies, and permissibility of monetary substitutions require qualified guidance.

What is Kaffarah for a deliberate broken oath?+

Quran 5:89 refers to feeding ten needy people, clothing ten or freeing a slave, with fasting three days if unable to use the applicable earlier options.

What does Kaffarah for a Ramadan fast violation involve?+

Sahih al-Bukhari 1936 describes a specific intercourse-while-fasting incident and a sequence involving freeing a slave, two successive lunar months or feeding sixty people as applicable. It does not apply indiscriminately to all broken fasts.

Can a broken oath require feeding ten people?+

Quran 5:89 lists feeding ten needy people or clothing them among its remedies. The number of distinct binding oaths and valid method require guidance.
