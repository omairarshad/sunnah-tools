Using Islamic planning tools thoughtfully

Use these planners to keep track of Quran reading, dhikr, fasting and daily prayers. Saved counts are for your own reference; they do not determine religious obligations.

Choose a realistic goal

A Quran reading plan can divide pages across available days, and a Tasbih counter can tally a personal dhikr target. Adjust goals to your circumstances. Different printed mushafs have different page counts, so enter the correct total for your own copy.

Saving prayer and fasting progress

The daily Salah checklist and fasting tracker use local browser storage. Clearing site data, changing devices or using a private window can remove saved progress. A printable prayer chart is a separate offline option for people who want a paper record.

Prepare for Hajj or Umrah

Use the packing checklist to organise documents, essentials and travel items. Verify visa, health, airline, baggage and pilgrimage requirements with the relevant official sources near your departure, since these rules can change. The checklist is a planning aid, not an official travel requirement list.

Know what the tools cannot decide

A tracker does not determine whether an individual owes make-up fasts or other obligations. A countdown does not confirm a moon-sighting announcement. For important religious questions, ask a qualified and trusted local authority.
