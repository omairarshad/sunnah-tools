Prayer times in New York City

New York’s boroughs span a broad area, so this page uses central-city coordinates. Check your exact location and preferred mosque’s Fajr and Isha calculation policy.

How the New York City times are calculated

We calculate times for New York City, United States, near 40.713° latitude and -74.006° longitude. The default calculation convention is North America / ISNA, which you can change. Your browser calculates the current local date, including daylight-saving changes where applicable.

Why times can differ

Prayer schedules vary when mosques use different Fajr or Isha angles, local corrections or a different Asr school. Calculated starts are not the same as jamaat/iqamah times. Check your chosen convention against your local mosque.

For Ramadan in New York City

Fajr is often used to check the start of fasting and Maghrib to check iftar. Confirm your fasting calendar and the first day of Ramadan with an appropriate local authority, since lunar dates can differ.
