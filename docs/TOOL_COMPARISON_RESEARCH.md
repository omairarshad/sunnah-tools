## Implementation priorities

1. **Tahajjud**: Support manual Maghrib and next-day Fajr, selectable city and convention, transparent last-third arithmetic and real overnight elapsed time. Benchmark [timeoftahajjud.com](https://timeoftahajjud.com/) and primary references [Sahih al-Bukhari 1145](https://sunnah.com/bukhari:1145), [Quran 17:79](https://quran.com/17/79).
2. **Prayer and calendars**: Explain local methods and moon-sighting differences, using [IslamicFinder](https://www.islamicfinder.org/world/united-kingdom/2643743/london-prayer-times/) and [timeanddate](https://www.timeanddate.com/calendar/islamic-calendar.html) as feature references.
3. **Zakat and compensation**: Emphasise net assets, locally verified rates, and alternatives rather than promising a ruling. Compare with [Islamic Relief](https://www.islamic-relief.org.uk/giving/islamic-giving/zakat/zakat-calculator/).
4. **Quran and trackers**: Practical reading ranges, careful verse references and private tracking, inspired by the available features on [Quran.com](https://quran.com/) and [Humble Halal](https://www.humblehalal.com/tools).

All calculations and sensitive religious guidance require their own validation; competitor content is not automatically authoritative. See `docs/CONTENT_REVIEW_REGISTER.md`.
